\begin{abstract}
Streaming generators produce video chunk by chunk. Each chunk conditions on
generated content. Errors therefore accumulate. Systems pin a reference to
slow this. Keeping that reference is unsolved. Frozen references go stale and
refreshed ones inherit drift. We keep the opening latent and re-expand it on a
schedule. The method is training-free. It adds no state. On matched controls
it retains identity better than frozen and refreshed references. The gain is
confined to identity. Total benchmark score is unchanged.
\end{abstract}
